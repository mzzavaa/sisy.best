Award Categories

Four categories recognizing different dimensions of impact, courage, and leadership.

Best Newcomer

The Rising Voice

For women who stepped onto their first stages in the past 12 months - and made it count. This award celebrates the courage of beginnings and the power of showing up for the first time.

  • First conference talk, podcast, or keynote
  • Nominated by community members or organizers
  • Includes mentorship package and SISY spotlight
Most Inspiring Voice

The Voice That Moves People

For the speaker whose words stay with you long after the event ends. This award recognizes the women who make audiences think, feel, and act differently.

  • Audience-nominated and community-voted
  • Based on talks, keynotes, or podcast appearances
  • Includes a featured profile on sisy.best
Changemaker of the Year

The Disruptor

For the woman who used her platform to challenge the status quo, drive systemic change, or shift how her industry thinks about a critical issue.

  • Evidence of measurable impact or shift
  • Editorial review by SISY advisory board
  • Includes press release and media kit
Industry Pioneer

The Trailblazer

For the woman who has spent years - or decades - opening doors, creating paths, and making it easier for the women who come after her.

  • Recognized for sustained career-long contribution
  • Nominated by peers and verified by editorial team
  • Lifetime achievement recognition and Hall of Fame listing

How Nominations Work

Simple, transparent, and community-powered. Anyone can nominate - and every nomination matters.

Step 1 - Nominate

Submit a nomination for any woman expert you believe deserves recognition. Describe her impact, her voice, and why she belongs on the list.

Step 2 - Community Vote

The SISY community reviews and votes on nominations. This ensures the list reflects the voices our community finds genuinely inspiring and impactful.

Step 3 - Editorial Review

The SISY editorial team reviews top nominations for accuracy and fit before announcing the final SISY 100 list each year.
